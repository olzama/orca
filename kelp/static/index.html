A node drawn with a dashed border is open: the remaining trees differ there. While many trees remain, the tree shown is the one that most of them agree with at each open node, and the grammar has not checked it. Once few remain, the grammar checks each one, drops those it does not license, and shows all the rest side by side in small form: open nodes are marked there (the highest one of each open part with its rule name; hover over a dot for the others), and the trees that the selected choice would remove are faded. Click a small tree to see it in full, with its meaning.
