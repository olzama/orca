Type an English sentence and get a complete grammar analysis with its meaning (MRS).

The idea behind the parser

Parsing as sequence labeling comes from our group, LyS at Universidade da Coruña: Carlos Gómez-Rodríguez and David Vilares, Constituent parsing as sequence labeling (EMNLP 2018). In Orca, a tagger labels each word of the sentence, the labels decode to a full HPSG derivation, and the grammar checks every result.

About

An orca is a fast dolphin, although not the fastest. Orca is inspired by DELPH-IN and builds on that community’s many years of work, including the English Resource Grammar (Flickinger 2000) and the ACE parser (Crysmann and Packard 2012) used on this page. Orca is developed with Claude. The code is under the MIT License, given in full at the bottom of this page.

?

Readings are the distinct meanings among the grammar’s best parses. A reading is shown only if its score is within the margin of the best one: at margin 1.25, its best parse is at least about 29% as likely as the top parse. A larger margin shows more readings; at most 1 reading shows only the top one.

Partial analysis

The grammar accepts no analysis of the whole sentence. These are the largest pieces of the tagger’s first guess that it does accept, each with its own meaning. They are not an analysis of the sentence and are never counted as one.

Derivation

      
Purple boxes are words, with their lexical type (gray) and lexical rules (purple). Blue boxes are syntactic rules; a box with several names is a chain of unary rules. Click any label to look it up in the grammar: what it is for, the types above and below it in the hierarchy, and its definition.

      

    


    

      
Feature structure

      
The whole sign the grammar built. A folded value shows a summary of what is inside; click it to unfold one level, ▾ to fold. Click a feature name to show only that value (the path above leads back). Values with nothing specified (bool, luk …) are hidden unless you ask. Purple numbers mark shared values; click one to light up where it occurs. A path such as SYNSEM LOCAL CAT HEAD opens directly. With ACE's comparison on, orange marks values that differ from ACE's sign.
