Orca demo

Type an English sentence. Orca proposes a derivation with a neural tagger, the English Resource Grammar checks it by replay and repairs it where it fails, the grammar's ranking model picks among the accepted derivations, and the result is a complete grammar analysis with its meaning (MRS).

Ctrl+Enter parses

Meaning

The meaning as MRS: one row per predicate, ordered by the words it comes from. Click a variable to see everywhere it occurs and what is known about it; click a predicate or a word to link the two.

Feature structure

      
The whole sign the grammar built, as an attribute–value matrix. Everything starts folded: click a type (or ▸) to unfold it and ▾ to fold it again. Purple numbers mark shared values (the same structure reached by several paths); click one to light up every place it occurs. A path such as SYNSEM LOCAL CAT HEAD opens directly.

      

      

    


    

      
Derivation

      
Purple boxes are words, with their lexical type (gray) and lexical rules (purple). Blue boxes are syntactic rules; a box with several names is a chain of unary rules.
