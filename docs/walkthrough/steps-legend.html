1. TagEach word gets a leaf label (lexical type and unary rules) and a tree label.
  2. DecodeThe tree labels say how each word joins the next. Together they fix exactly one tree.
  3. Look upEach word's lexical entry is found in the grammar's lexicon from its type and rules.
  4. ReplayBottom-up, the grammar unifies each rule with its daughters. One pass, no search.
  5. RepairIf replay fails, the tagger's next-best labels under the failing node are tried. Up to 30 accepted derivations are collected.
  6. RankThe ranking model that ships with the grammar, the one ACE uses, picks the most likely of them.
  7. Read offThe result is the full sign. Its semantics is written out as MRS and DMRS.
